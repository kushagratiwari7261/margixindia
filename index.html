Margix Pvt Ltd | Unified Business Suite & Logistics Platform

One online platform where an Indian business runs its daily work (billing, stock, purchases, accounts, GST) and moves its goods by truck, without needing Tally or separate software.

Business Suite (9 Apps)

  • Sales and Billing: Quotations, sales orders, GST tax invoices, dynamic UPI QR codes.
  • Purchase: Vendor orders, goods received notes (GRN), bills, 3-way matching.
  • Inventory: Multi-godown tracking, batch/expiry, machine serial numbers, barcode package labels.
  • Accounts & GST: Complete double-entry books replacing Tally, native e-invoice, e-way bills, GSTR-1/3B.
  • Parties Master: Single directory with automatic GSTIN verification and credit limits.
  • Owner Dashboard & Reports: Real-time cash, debtor aging, stock valuation, Excel export.
  • Team Administration: Granular role-based controls and complete audit trails.
  • Ship by Truck: Direct bridge from sales invoices to freight loads.
  • WhatsApp Catalogue: Mobile commerce links landing as draft sales orders.

Logistics Platform (6 Apps)

  • Load Marketplace: Verified load quoting and matching for Indian lanes.
  • Transport Operations (FTL): Full truckloads, sequential digital LRs, driver advances.
  • Branch & Godown (PTL): Part-truckload hub staging, delivery runs, cash on delivery (COD).
  • Fleet & Driver Compliance: RC, fitness, insurance, permit alerts and expiry locks.
  • 3PL Capacity Pool: Shared trucks and automated return backhaul matching.
  • Margix Driver Mobile App: Offline-first checkpoints, photo proof of delivery (POD), digital signature.
